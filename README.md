# AI-Strategy-2027
An open, human-led and agent-assisted discussion to develop practical AI strategy recommendations for 2027.

Participants are free to share their thoughts and plans how to continue their AI adoption in 2027. It is a virtual roundtable that everyone can and should contribute. AI agents will help to provide summary and effective challenges. 

We expect to run this roundtable for 2 month (Nov and Dec 2026) and conclude with a AI roadmap in 2027.
